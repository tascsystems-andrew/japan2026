Day 2 — One Weather Choice

Fuji or Science, Then Hamamatsu

Everyone stays together today. If Fuji was not properly seen yesterday and the morning is clear, use Nihondaira Yume Terrace. If Fuji was already seen or the weather is poor, use the hands-on Ru·Ku·Ru science museum beside Shizuoka Station. Never do both, and skip the whole branch if it threatens the booked 12:21 train.

The Morning Branch
Option A · clear retry

Nihondaira Yume Terrace

Leave after breakfast in three taxis and aim for roughly 09:00–10:00 at the viewpoint. This is the second chance only if yesterday did not deliver a proper Fuji view. Return to Kuretake around 10:30 for the bags.

Shizuoka Science Museum Ru·Ku·Ru

Check out, store the bags and use the hands-on museum beside the station from about 09:30–11:15. This is the indoor fallback, not an add-on to Nihondaira. Collect the bags and buy a quick lunch before the train.
