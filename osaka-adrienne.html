Umeda — glasses, takoyaki, shops

Connie's glasses, a make-your-own takoyaki lunch, the Umeda shops, and a sake cellar under a floor on the way home.

Day 16

A deliberately lighter one

Adrienne arrived from Tokyo last night on the Green Car shinkansen, in time for Sam's birthday. Today is north Osaka: Connie's progressives are ready for collection at Grand Front, Tako no Tetsu does the make-your-own takoyaki lunch, and Umeda is the shopping. It finishes at a liquor shop in Nishi-ku with a cellar under the floor.

This day used to be Amerikamura, Shinsekai and Dotonbori — but Shinsekai and Dotonbori were both done on 7 November, and repeating them thirteen days later with the same people is a wasted day. Minami moved to 28 November, when Sam, Adrienne and the children do it properly. Today is the Umeda errand day, and it is the lighter for it.

Takoyaki Lunch — Tako no Tetsu

Umeda · KITTE Osaka 4F · booked for 12:00

A restaurant where you make your own takoyaki at the table. They bring the batter, the octopus, the toppings and a hot griddle, and you do the rest. Somebody shows you the pour, the fill and the wrist-flick that turns them in the mould, and then you are on your own. You eat what you make.

It is messy, funny, and doubles as entertainment and lunch. Everyone will finish with their own technique and strong opinions about whose turned out best.

🧒 Kids love: pouring the batter, choosing toppings, and the challenge of flipping the balls with chopsticks — harder than it looks.

Booked: nine people across two matching seat-only, non-smoking table reservations: four people including two children and five people including two children. Nearby tables were requested as one group but are not guaranteed. KITTE Osaka 4F, 06-6345-0301. This make-your-own takoyaki lunch is included in the tour. No cancellation fee, including same-day cancellation; online changes and cancellation are free.

14:00

Umeda — the shops

Kita Ward · Grand Front, Lucua, HEP, Hankyu

All of it is within a few minutes of Osaka Station and most of it is under cover. Nine people will not stay together and should not try — agree a time and a place and let everyone go.

Tip: from 1 November 2026 in-store tax-free has ended. You pay full price and claim the refund at the airport, landside, before you check your bags.

🚃 Umeda → Hommachi → Awaza (Chuo line), about ten minutes. Awaza exit 2, four minutes on foot
17:00

Shimada Shoten — the hatch in the floor

3-5-1 Itachibori, Nishi-ku · closes 18:30

A liquor shop that has sold sake since 1954, with a cellar underneath that you climb down into through a hatch in the floor. Three hundred-odd bottles down there, tasting about ¥222 a glass with a snack, thirty minutes maximum. Photographs allowed, no phone calls. There is English information and staff who can help.

This is Dan's. Two of his quests land here and both windows are today only — the aged koshu, and the bottle for the rectangle of space he left in his case before he flew.

Tip: it closes at 18:30, so break off the shopping by 17:45. Awaza is one stop from Kujo, so it is on the way home rather than a detour. 06-6531-8119.
