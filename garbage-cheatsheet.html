🗑️ Garbage Guide

Konohana Ward, Osaka City
Kasugadekita 2-chōme · Osaka 554-0021
Three rules: Use transparent bags only (no coloured/opaque bags). Put garbage out in the morning on collection day (not the night before). Place at the designated collection spot (ask host if unsure).
Mon & Thu
月 ・ 木

Regular Garbage

普通ごみ（ふつうごみ）

Kitchen scraps (squeeze out water), paper waste, diapers (bag separately), small electronics, toys, shoes, glass, ceramics, furniture under 30cm.

Wrap sharp items in paper → write 危険 (danger) on bag
Tuesday
火

Paper & Clothing

古紙衣類（こしいるい）

Newspapers, cardboard, paper cartons, magazines, misc paper — tie each type separately with string in a cross shape. Clothing goes in a transparent bag (washed & dried).

Keep types separate — don't mix cardboard with newspapers
Wednesday
水

Plastic Resources

プラスチック資源

Anything with the プラ mark (packaging, trays, bags, bottles, foam). Also 100% plastic products (utensils, storage bins, toothbrushes, toys). Rinse food residue off. If too dirty → regular garbage.

Remove caps from bottles. Look for the プラ symbol.
Saturday
土

Resource Garbage

資源ごみ（しげんごみ）

Cans, bottles, PET bottles & small metal items (pots, pans, foil) — all together in one transparent bag. Rinse, remove caps, crush PET bottles. Spray cans go in a separate bag (use up contents, no need to puncture).

PET bottles: remove cap + label → crush → bag with cans/bottles

⛔ Don't put these in any garbage

Lithium batteries & power banks → drop at public facility collection boxes. Gasoline, paint, fire extinguishers, car tires → not collected by the city. Large items over 30cm → call for oversized pickup.

Quick Reference

PET Bottle Steps

🧴1. Empty & rinse
2. Remove cap → plastic bag (Wed)
3. Remove label → plastic bag (Wed)
4. Crush bottle → resource bag (Sat)

Cooking Oil

🍳Soak into paper/cloth, add water to prevent combustion, double-bag it → regular garbage (Mon/Thu)

Oversized Items

📦Anything over 30cm or over 3 bags at once = oversized (粗大ごみ). Call to book: 0120-79-0053
